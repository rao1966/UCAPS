## 1. Overview

The Universal Cyber Attack Prediction System (UCAPS) is a proposed research framework for investigating predictive cyber defence.

Contemporary cybersecurity systems provide important capabilities for threat detection, security monitoring, incident response and recovery. However, forecasting potential cyber-attacks before exploitation remains a challenging research problem.

UCAPS proposes an integrated architecture combining threat intelligence, vulnerability intelligence, behavioural analytics, cyber knowledge graphs, attack graph analytics, artificial intelligence and simulation-assisted defensive planning.

The central research objective is to investigate whether these capabilities can be integrated to support more informed and proactive cyber defence.

**Research status:** UCAPS is currently a conceptual research proposal. The framework has not yet been established as an implemented or experimentally validated operational system.

## 2. Research Objectives

UCAPS investigates three complementary prediction objectives:

- **P1 – Vulnerability Exploitation Forecasting:** Investigate the likelihood of vulnerabilities being exploited within a defined context and prediction horizon.
- **P2 – Enterprise Asset Attack Forecasting:** Investigate which enterprise assets may become potential targets of cyber-attacks.
- **P3 – Next Attack Step or Path Forecasting:** Investigate potential subsequent attack actions and paths using contextual and graph-based analysis.

These objectives are intended to be studied individually and collectively.

## 3. Proposed Six-Layer Architecture

The original UCAPS proposal defines six principal layers:

| Layer | Component | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Threat Intelligence | Gather external cyber threat intelligence |
| L2 | Data Collection | Collect relevant enterprise security information |
| L3 | Behaviour Analytics | Examine activities, patterns and anomalies |
| L4 | Cyber Knowledge Graph | Represent relationships among assets, vulnerabilities, threats and events |
| L5 | Attack Graph Analytics | Analyse possible attack paths and dependencies |
| L6 | Prediction and Simulation | Investigate attack forecasting and defensive scenarios |

The architecture is a proposed design. Its components and integration require systematic implementation and evaluation.

## 4. Research Components

The research explores:

- Threat and vulnerability intelligence integration.
- Behavioural and contextual analysis.
- Cyber knowledge graph construction.
- Attack graph modelling and analysis.
- Artificial intelligence-based prediction.
- Attack probability and exploitability estimation.
- Potential target-asset identification.
- Attack-path forecasting.
- Cyber Digital Twin concepts and simulation-assisted defensive planning.

## 5. Research Methodology and Development

The project follows an evidence-oriented, iterative research approach:

1. Conceptual framework and theoretical formulation.
2. Architecture refinement and practical implementation.
3. Controlled experiments and validation.
4. Comparison with appropriate existing approaches and baselines.
5. Final assessment of findings, limitations and future research directions.

The sequence may evolve in response to technical findings, external feedback and experimental evidence.

## 7. Research Status and Limitations

At this stage:

- The six-layer architecture represents the original conceptual proposal.
- Prediction models and mathematical formulations require further examination.
- Practical implementation and experimental validation remain future research activities.
- No operational prediction accuracy or attack-prevention effectiveness is claimed.
- Universal applicability and guaranteed cyber-attack prevention are not claimed.

Research modifications will be documented with their technical rationale and supporting evidence.
